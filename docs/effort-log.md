session. Human hours are Steve's own time (design, review, testing), logged
honestly; leave blank rather than guess.

Human hours are transcript-derived estimates of Steve's active time, not
stopwatch measurements. Each row counts Steve's own messages in the session
transcripts (and his answers to questions and plan approvals), assigned to
the release committed next ("publish rNN" to rNN). Time between two of his
messages counts in full when the gap is 30 minutes or less, and as 5 minutes
(reading and replying) when longer, so breaks don't count. It only sees time
around the conversation: testing on his own appliance between messages
counts when the next message came within 30 minutes. Two rows are Steve's own
estimates, not derived from a transcript: 0.5 h for reviewing the code with
ChatGPT (0.9.9-r02), and 0.5 h for the 2026-11-21 review follow-ups. Where a row's notes say "measured", they
